Scorza Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Scorza, a score keeping app for Android, handles information. Scorza is made by its independent developer ("we").

Data we do not collect

Scorza has no accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store or receive:

  • your players, teams, photos, matches, notes or statistics;
  • your contacts, location or files.

All of that stays in the app's private storage on your device. Android cloud backup is turned off for Scorza, so this data is not copied to Google Drive.

Internet connection

Scorza needs an internet connection to run because it shows ads. The connection is used only by the Google services described below; your match data is never sent anywhere.

Data handled by third parties

Scorza uses two Google services that connect to the internet:

  1. Google AdMob shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads to users who have not bought Remove Ads. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your IP address, coarse location derived from your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data, to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the app. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  2. Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app only checks, on your device, whether your Google account owns the purchase.

Backups and sharing

Backups are files you create and save wherever you choose. Sharing a result sends an image made on your device to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. We never receive these files.

Children

Scorza is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Your data → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall it. Ad data held by Google is governed by Google's policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
