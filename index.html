Scorza Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how Scorza, a score keeping app for iPhone, iPad and Android, handles information. Scorza is made by its independent developer ("we").

Data we do not collect

Scorza has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store or receive:

  • your players, teams, photos, matches, notes, statistics or settings;
  • your contacts, location or files.

All of that stays in the app's private storage on your device. On iOS it is excluded from iCloud backups; on Android, cloud backup is turned off for Scorza.

Internet connection

Scorza uses the internet only for ads and purchases. Your match data is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

Users who have not bought Remove Ads see banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, with consent managed by Google's User Messaging Platform. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address and coarse location derived from it;
  • advertising data such as ads shown and tapped;
  • app interaction, crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. On iOS you are also asked through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings (Ad privacy choices on iOS, Privacy options on Android), and on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether the purchase is owned. After purchase, no ads are requested.

Backups and sharing

Backups are files you create and save wherever you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive). Sharing a result creates an image on your device and hands it to the share sheet; you decide where it goes. We never receive these files.

Children

Scorza is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Use Delete all data in the app's settings, or delete the app. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side. Ad data held by Google is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
